National Electrical Code (NFPA 70) — Conduit Standards

Field-bending rules, minimum radii, pull box sizing & support intervals

NEC 358.26 / 344.26 / 342.26

Bends — Number in One Run (Maximum 360° Rule)

There shall not be more than the equivalent of four quarter bends (360 degrees total) between pull points, for example, conduit bodies and boxes.

💡 Field installation rule: Always install a junction box, pull box, or conduit body (LB/C/T) before cumulative bends exceed 360° to avoid tearing conductor insulation during pull.
NEC 358.24 & Chapter 9, Table 2

Bends — How Made & Minimum Centerline Radii

Bends shall be made so that the conduit will not be damaged and the internal diameter will not be effectively reduced. Minimum centerline bend radii for one-shot / shoe benders:

NEC 314.28

Pull and Junction Boxes Minimum Dimensions

For straight pulls: minimum box length = 8 × trade diameter. For angle or U-pulls: distance to opposite wall = 6 × trade diameter plus additional conduits.

NEC 358.30

Securing and Supporting

Conduit shall be securely fastened within 3 ft (900 mm) of each outlet box or termination, and supported at least every 10 ft (3.05 m).
